# BookSwap

BookSwap is a comprehensive peer-to-peer book marketplace and community platform that enables readers to buy, sell, trade, and lend books while fostering a vibrant reading community through online book clubs and social features.

The platform addresses the inefficiency of idle books sitting on shelves while readers actively search for affordable reading materials. By combining marketplace functionality with community building and AI-powered features, BookSwap creates a sustainable ecosystem for book enthusiasts.
